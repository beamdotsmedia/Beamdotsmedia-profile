### Must-cover topics (on 2+ competitor pages)

- What's included: campaign setup and structure, audience targeting + retargeting, creative/copy testing, **Meta Pixel + Conversions API tracking**, reporting.
- Process steps (both agency pages show 4 steps).
- FAQ: how much to spend · boost vs real ads · WhatsApp ads · do I need a website.
- Cost explained as **ad spend (paid to Meta) vs management fee**, plus CPC/CPM/CPL basics.

### Gaps = our opportunity

1. **Real pricing in RM.** None of the agency pages show any. This is the biggest gap and what searchers want.
2. **Real results.** No competitor service page shows numbers. We have Verthys 12x ROAS and Chillins 8x ROAS.
3. **Malaysia-specific cost guidance:** a minimum test budget in RM, typical CPL ranges by industry from our own accounts, and the ROAS to expect.
4. **Click-to-WhatsApp ads.** This is how Malaysian SMEs actually close deals; only the freelancer mentions it.
5. **Industries named and linked to proof:** beauty/aesthetics, fashion/lifestyle e-commerce, F&B, furniture/interior.
6. **Bilingual EN + 中文 version.** Only NK has one.
7. **Local signal:** PJ / Klang Valley, in person or remote.

### Target length

Competitor median is ~300–650 words. Our page has ~400 words of body text now.
→ **Target 900–1,100 words EN** of body content (above everyone without bloat). Match it in the ZH twin.

### Angle

> "The only Malaysian Meta ads agency page that shows real RM costs and real ROAS results, so you know what you'd spend and what to expect before you book a call."

Pricing is **case by case**, so no RM figures are published. The cost H2 explains the cost structure, what drives it and how we quote, and uses our real ROAS results as the "what to expect" proof. Trade-off: this ranks less strongly for "facebook ads cost malaysia" than a page with RM ranges would, but the primary target ("agency" + PJ) is unaffected. The angle shifts to:
